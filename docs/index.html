01 / matching

confidence, not guesses.

Read embedded metadata, infer from filenames, and check external catalogs. Uncertain matches stay open for your confirmation.

▤
02 / structure

series stay in order.

Authors, series, volume numbers, editions and custom shelves; organized without touching the books on disk.

◷
03 / history

remember what you read.

Started, finished, abandoned, reread. Keep dates, minutes, notes, ratings and individual reading sessions.

⇩
04 / ownership

leave with your data.

Export structured JSON or Goodreads-shaped CSV for StoryGraph and other supported import workflows.

02 / the workflow

three steps.
less bookkeeping.

The index is yours. Nothing gets moved, renamed or deleted.

01

point it at a folder.

Recursive scans gather metadata from supported ebooks, starting with EPUB and PDF.

bookledger scan-folder ~/Books
02

check the uncertain bits.

Review missing series numbers, ambiguous editions and low-confidence classifications.

bookledger serve
03

keep a record.

Track your reading and export whenever you want. SQLite stays on your machine.

bookledger export json library.json
03 / start here

your next chapter
starts locally.

Python, SQLite, and a web interface that opens in your browser. No account, no sync service, no AI.
